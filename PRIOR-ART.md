# Scope and prior work

- Coulter and Do give the cut–join–flip recursion (Corollary 4.16), the original connected matching-word interpretation (Proposition 4.18), and the general real-rootedness and interlacing questions (Conjectures 4.21 and 4.22). Their conjectural parameter domain excludes $b=0,-1$. This work treats connected one-part rows in genus $1/2$. It proves exact homogeneity in $b$, identifies those rows with the known Wishart polynomials, and proves strict negative-root interlacing and the resulting weak interlacing of the original rows. [Coulter–Do, section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.04002v1#S4)
- Dumitriu and Edelman's real-Laguerre correction measure gives the same shape polynomial as its moment, using two endpoint atoms and an arcsine measure. This refers to the signed measure in their Theorem 1.5, with the separate beta-dependent prefactor stripped off. [Dumitriu–Edelman](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0510043)
- Mingo and Vazquez-Becerra's Proposition 19 gives the same coefficients ($(\binom{2m}{2j}-\binom{m}{j}^2)/2$) as an annular noncrossing-permutation count. Their Theorems 42, 43 and 45 give recurrence and generating-function descriptions. Arizmendi, Garza-Vargas and Perales obtain the negative of that moment polynomial in their Laguerre Example 5.9, equation (38). [Mingo–Vazquez-Becerra](https://arxiv.org/html/2112.15231v2) · [Arizmendi–Garza-Vargas–Perales](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.08489v2)
- The orthogonal-polynomial proof uses the classical Gegenbauer differential and adjacent-degree identities. For parameter $1$ the quotient difference is proportional to an ordinary Gegenbauer polynomial, so interlacing in that special case follows from the classical theory. The result here treats the consecutive difference for every fixed positive parameter. [NIST DLMF, Chapter 18](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18)
- The companion genus-two release treats only two two-part profiles. The five shared matching-word definitions retain their attribution and license. [Genus-two release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24557177)
