# Connected one-part matching words in genera zero and one half

For every positive degree d, every connected monotone matching word for the one-part profile (d) in genus 1/2 has precisely one flip. In genus zero every such word has zero flips. Consequently the genus-1/2 row at b is b times the row at b=1, including b=0 and b=−1. These assertions concern actual labeled words in Proposition 4.18. The numerical finite-hive recurrence, its formal generating function, the orthogonal-polynomial change of variables and the full root theorem below are independently checked.

The definitions in Hurwitz are the original one-based, connected, endpoint-constrained matching-word definitions from the previously verified two-profile project. The five imported local sources are included byte for byte. The parameter m in the new module names denotes degree d=m+1.

- `HalfWords.raw_cyclic_eq_dp` identifies the genuine raw one-part sum with the chronological finite sum at every allowed word length. `endpoint_connected` proves why transitivity follows from the endpoint and the initial one-part condition.
- `HalfWords.actual_step` classifies each actual swap of distinct labels, for every pair of fixed-point-free involutions on an arbitrary ordered finite set. The component count is the cardinality of the quotient by alternating reachability and includes standard pairs. A cut increases that count by one and has charge cost zero. A flip preserves all component supports and has cost one. A join decreases the count by one.
- `HalfWords.word_excess` shows that an accepted word of length r starting at C components and ending at D components has D≤r+C. For r+C≤D+1, its original total charge equals r+C−D. It holds independently of monotonicity. Its application to the original hive-weighted sum is `HalfWords.original_word_homogeneous`; `original_degree_one` and `original_zero_parameter` give exact degenerate rows.
- `HalfWords.unmatched_pivot` gives the exact largest-hive recurrence for the original bounded word sums. For a nonstandard exposed pair (h,s(h)) the sole shrink correction has coefficient t−1. Its unique child swaps h with M(s(h)), on the same component and with charge zero. The identity holds in any commutative ring and specializes at t=0.
- `HalfWords.active_recurrence` specializes the live word recurrence to the two small excesses, suppressing all unrelated joins. `sum_internal_labels` identifies its lower labels exactly with two copies of the orbit minus its pivot. For every function of the nontrivial part sizes, `actual_cut_profile_sum` counts each ordered split once, `actual_flip_profile_sum` gives L−1 copies of the old profile, and `actual_correction_profile` evaluates the child in the t−1 correction. The length-two and singleton conventions are included. These labels come from arbitrary actual matching coordinates for the standard and current matchings. Positive and negative representatives outside the pivot component contribute zero for both excesses.
- `hive_initially_active` proves on actual chronological words that a hive can only occur on a pair which was nonstandard at the beginning. `fresh_restrict` discards all initially paired upper hives at once. `unbounded_recurrence` thus expresses the unrestricted genuine row entirely in unrestricted child rows; each child has one fewer transposition. `active_max` and `active_coordinates` give the actual maximal eligible pivot and its canonically negative representative.
- `true_profile` proves the unrestricted fresh word value at either budget, for every labeled initial matching. It is the appropriate component of the product of dual-number rows over all alternating parts. The proof is an actual induction on word length. `original_word_jet` specializes it to the connected one-part word sum in Proposition 4.18. For every m≥1 the genus-one-half raw sum is b times the second entry of `jetRow t m`. The dual-number recurrence specializes to the two recurrences of Corollary 4.16. `exact_generating_function` proves the endpoint-minus-arcsine formal series. `pullback` identifies the true polynomial after the fractional-linear change with the Chebyshev-minus-Legendre difference, including the degree and scalar normalization.
- `HalfWords.hurwitz_consecutive` proves the original genus-one-half all-degree root and interlacing theorem for every nonzero real b at successive part sizes d+2,d+3, including the empty lower list d=0. It gives the full root multisets, natural degrees, splitting, simple zero at zero and strictly alternating simple negative roots. `zero_mark_row` and `degree_one_row` prove both degenerate families. `HalfGenus.consecutive_differences` proves the full auxiliary normalized Gegenbauer difference theorem for every positive real λ. For every real b and every pair of natural numbers m,j, `HalfWords.hurwitz_coefficient` proves that the coefficient of t^j in the original row with part size m+1 is b/(2(m+1)) times the difference of binom(2m+2,2j) and binom(m+1,j)^2. The two binomial recurrences in `HalfWords.BinomialRows` and the coefficient extraction in `HalfWords.BinomialFormula` also give the exact polynomial identity `HalfWords.hurwitz_binomial_polynomial`.
